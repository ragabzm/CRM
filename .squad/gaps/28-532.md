# Gaps found while implementing Story 11.1 (Azure #532)

Both of these are outside this story's scope and were left alone.

## `TOUCH_TARGET` hit areas overlap when controls are stacked close together

`lib/utils.ts` extends a small control's tap area to 44px with an
`after:size-11` pseudo-element centred on it. Two controls stacked `gap-3`
(12px) apart therefore have overlapping 44px areas, and the one later in DOM
order wins.

Measured on `/portal/feedback` at a 500px viewport: `document.elementFromPoint`
8px below the centre-bottom of the 👍 button returns the **"add a comment"**
button, not the rating button.

Consequence is mild where it was found — the rating is already recorded by the
time that link appears, so a low tap opens the comment box instead of doing
nothing — but the pattern is used across the product on rows of small controls
where the wrong neighbour may matter more.

Not fixed here because it is a change to a shared utility every screen uses,
and picking the right fix (padding instead of a pseudo-element, or a smaller
area where controls are close) is a UX decision, not a Story 11.1 decision.

## `after:-z-10` can put a tap target behind an opaque background

The same utility paints its extended area at `-z-10`. Inside a container with
an opaque background painted in a higher stacking context, that area is covered
and the extension silently does nothing — no error, no visual difference, just
a control that is harder to hit than it looks. Not observed failing on the
screens touched by this story; recorded because it cannot be seen by looking.
